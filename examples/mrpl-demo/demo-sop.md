# DEMO-SOP-001 — Inspection review workflow

SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. This is not an MRPL standard or an engineering acceptance criterion.

Revision A. Intended only for the supplied demonstration records.

1. Record each equipment tag, source file, page, observed condition, measurement method, value and unit.
2. A measurement with no unit is incomplete. Request clarification; do not guess the unit.
3. Visible staining is an observation, not confirmation of a leak source or rate.
4. Measured thickness cannot establish fitness for service without an applicable design basis and an approved acceptance limit. Neither is supplied in this demonstration.
5. Compare revisions against matching equipment, measurement point and method. Preserve both original readings and identify the revision.
6. Prepare a draft note for human review with proposed investigation actions. Do not grant approval, infer a cost, assign an owner, or invent a due date.
7. Record missing cost estimates, units, acceptance limits, ownership and approval as unresolved.
8. The action tracker must preserve source references and keep engineering decisions pending human review.
