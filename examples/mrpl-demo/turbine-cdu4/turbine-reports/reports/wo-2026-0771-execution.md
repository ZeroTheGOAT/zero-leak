# WO-2026-0771 — execution record, line 4-P-1102 elbow spool

interpretation or engineering acceptance.

References: Approval Note of 20 March 2026 (replacement approved, PR-88431),
thickness-log.csv (round 1, 14 March 2026), SHUTDOWN-WINDOW-2026 (window
12–26 September 2026).

## Sequence of work

- 2026-09-18: line 4-P-1102-6in-CS isolated, drained and gas-freed (first 48
  hours of the window). Old elbow spool containing test point TP-04 cut out.
  The cut-out spool measured 7.1 mm at TP-04 against the 9.8 mm retirement
  limit of ENG-STD-221 — consistent with the March survey. The spool was
  retained for metallurgical examination.
- 2026-09-19: new spool fitted. Material per PR-88431: ASTM A106 Grade B
  seamless, NPS 6 Schedule 40, long radius elbow plus one metre of straight
  spool. Both new welds completed and UT-surveyed.
- 2026-09-20: radiography of both new welds carried out before the line was
  returned to service, as required by the approval note. Both welds were
  accepted; no reportable indications were found. Radiograph IDs
  RT-2026-0912-A and RT-2026-0912-B are filed with this work order.

## Observations for review

- TP-04 on the old spool read 7.1 mm in March 2026 and is now removed; the new
  spool reads 12.5 mm of its 12.7 mm nominal.
- TP-02 measured 9.9 mm — the lowest remaining reading on the line and only
  0.1 mm above the 9.8 mm retirement limit. Loss since the previous survey is
  0.5 mm, below the 2.0 mm interval-halving trigger of SOP-INSP-014, but the
  point remains the one to watch at the next six-monthly survey.
- No reading on the line is at or below a retirement limit after this window;
  no Form MEC-DEV-07 deviation report is required for this survey.
