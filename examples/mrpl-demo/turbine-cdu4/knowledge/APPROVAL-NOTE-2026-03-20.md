# Approval Note — Spool replacement, line 4-P-1102
Date: 20 March 2026
Raised by: Inspection Department, following Form MEC-UT-11 dated 14 March 2026
Approved by: Chief Inspector (Mechanical), MRPL Mangalore

## Decision
The elbow spool on line 4-P-1102-6in-CS is approved for replacement. Test point TP-04
measured 7.1 mm against a retirement limit of 9.8 mm and is therefore below the acceptance
criterion of ENG-STD-221.

## Commercial
Purchase requisition PR-88431 raised against budget code CDU4-MECH-11 for a value of
INR 4,20,000. Material: ASTM A106 Grade B seamless, NPS 6 Schedule 40, long radius elbow
plus one metre of straight spool.

Work order WO-2026-0771. To be executed in the next available shutdown window. Radiography
of both new welds is mandatory before the line is returned to service.
