# SOP-INSP-014 — Ultrasonic Wall Thickness Survey
Revision 6, effective 01 January 2026. Owner: Inspection Department, MRPL Mangalore.

## 1. Scope
Applies to all carbon steel piping in CDU-4, VDU-2 and the FCC feed circuit, sizes 2 inch
through 24 inch, in hydrocarbon service below 400 degC.

## 2. Instrument and calibration
Only a Krautkramer DM5E or Olympus 38DL-Plus may be used. The instrument shall be
calibrated against calibration block CAL-BLK-V1 at the start and end of every survey.
Couplant shall be glycerine paste; water is not acceptable above 60 degC.
Probe frequency shall be 5 MHz for wall thickness below 20 mm.

## 3. Test points
At every condition monitoring location four readings shall be taken at the 12, 3, 6 and 9
o'clock quadrants. The lowest of the four is recorded as the measured thickness. A test
point is identified as TP-nn and its position shall be permanently marked with low-stress
stamping.

## 4. Reporting
Results are recorded on Form MEC-UT-11. Any reading at or below the retirement limit
requires a deviation report on Form MEC-DEV-07 raised within 24 hours to the Chief
Inspector, and the line shall be placed on the six-monthly monitoring list.
A loss of more than 2.0 mm since the previous survey requires the interval to be halved.


## Revision 2

Revision 2 adds: any reading below 6.0 mm on a CDU-4 line must be reported to the Chief Inspector within 24 hours.


## Revision 2

Revision 2 adds: any reading below 6.0 mm on a CDU-4 line must be reported to the Chief Inspector within 24 hours, and the line derated to 70 percent of design pressure until a spool is cut out.
