# ENG-STD-221 — Corrosion Allowance and Retirement Limits
Revision 3, 15 February 2025. Owner: Engineering Services, MRPL Mangalore.

## Basis
Retirement limit = nominal wall thickness minus the corrosion allowance, subject to the
minimum structural thickness of the schedule.

## Allowances by service
| Service | Corrosion allowance | Review interval |
|---|---|---|
| CDU overhead, wet H2S | 3.0 mm | 6 months |
| CDU overhead, dry | 1.6 mm | 24 months |
| VDU transfer line | 2.5 mm | 12 months |
| Utility water | 1.0 mm | 36 months |

## Worked value for 6 inch Schedule 40 carbon steel
Nominal wall 12.7 mm at NPS 6 Schedule 40 (ASME B36.10M). In CDU overhead wet H2S service
the allowance is 3.0 mm, giving a retirement limit of 9.7 mm; the department rounds up to
9.8 mm for reporting. Below the retirement limit the spool shall be replaced, not repaired
by weld overlay, because wet H2S service is susceptible to hydrogen-induced cracking.
